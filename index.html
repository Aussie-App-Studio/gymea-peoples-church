Who We Are

A local fellowship rooted in Scripture and community

Christian

Who We Are & What We Believe

Grounded in God’s Word, devoted to His people

Gymea Peoples Church is an independent, evangelical local church in Gymea Bay committed to historic Christian faith, loving families, and supporting global missions.

We Are:

Christian

We worship and follow Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour. We identify with the historic Christian faith, believing that Jesus Christ came from Heaven, lived upon earth with the express purpose of dying in man’s place for man’s sin, and was raised from the dead. We believe He offers salvation to all who will repent of their sin and put their trust in him. We endeavour, with God’s help, to live our lives as Christian people by following the example of Jesus’ life.

Non-denominational

We believe in the autonomy of each local Christian fellowship, preferring to take full responsibility for all matters relating to the organisation and administration of our worship and life together. We do not criticise or condemn others who worship in denominational churches. However, we believe God is more interested in our personal relationship with Him than in any nominal church affiliation.

Evangelical

We believe that the Bible is God’s inspired Word to us and is our authority in all matters of faith and practice. We believe that every individual needs to have his or her relationship with God restored by placing his or her faith in Jesus Christ. We believe it is our responsibility to share this message of faith and hope to all people everywhere.

Family-oriented

The Gymea Peoples Church is very supportive of families. We believe that the family is an institution of God’s design, and we seek to support and strengthen the family at every opportunity. We conduct many activities so that families can be together and grow together.

Missionary-minded

In obedience to Jesus’ command, we support the sharing of the Gospel (good news) to all nations. Some of our own members are serving the Lord in PNG and the Ukraine, and we support other missionaries and mission organisations at home and abroad.

Our Purpose

Aims & Objectives

01

To fulfil Christ’s last command to His followers – to tell all people of the good news that His death for our sins will bring peace with God for all who believe.

02

To maintain a place of worship in Gymea so that men and women, boys and girls, may have a place where they can go to meet with God and other people of like faith.

03

To be involved in the task of worldwide missions.

04

To enjoy each other’s friendship and company as we seek to encourage one another to serve the Lord.

Rzhyshchiv, Ukraine

Daniel & Priscilla Gollan

Commissioned from GPC in 1991, Daniel and Priscilla have served in Ukraine for over 30 years — planting 3 churches and shepherding a congregation of ~80 in Rzhyshchiv while loving refugees, crisis families, and the addicted.

Family: Grown children: Jesse, Marie, Angel, Elizabeth. Daniel married Priscilla, daughter of U.S. missionaries to Ukraine, and they have spent the last 22 years anchored in Rzhyshchiv (population ~10,000).

Contact: danieljgollan@gmail.com · YouTube: @dangollan8329 · Phone: +380 63 617 3619
Mailing: 2 Nezalezhnostry St, Rzhishchiv 09230, Ukraine

Mindanao, Philippines

Jason & Shirley Birkin

Serving with Ethnos NZ among the Tala-andig people since the 1980s, helping third-generation believers read the Torah and New Testament on mobile phones while advancing Old Testament translation.

In the 1980s Jason and Shirley pioneered a gospel witness among the Tala-andig people. Today Tala-andig believers are reaching their own people through teaching the grand narrative of the Bible alongside ongoing Old Testament translation, discipleship, and community development.

Contact: jason.birkin@ntcp.ph · shirley.birkin@ntcp.ph

Central Mindanao, Philippines

Ron & Michelle Jennings

Serving with New Tribes Church Planters of the Philippines since 1981, pioneering outreach to the Higa-unon tribal people — putting their spoken language into writing and translating Scripture.

With the Higa-unon church now well-established and led by local leaders and tribal evangelists, Ron and Michelle established a printery and small hydro project and are completing Old Testament translation (currently translating Ezekiel), with completed Scriptures available on a smartphone Bible app.

Family: Three grown daughters (Robyn, Natalie, and Stephanie) living in Australia.
Email: ron_michelle_jennings@tribalministry.com

Bohol, Philippines

Stephen & Grace Cochrane (IGM)

Serving with Island Gospel Ministries in Talibon, Bohol — partnering with an association of 13 island churches, Galilee campsite, high school student boarding, and local church leadership training.

Begun by Bill and Julie Cochrane in 1986 as an outreach to the offshore islands of Bohol, Steve has served since 2005 (marrying Grace in 2013) alongside faithful Filipino co-workers Pastor Junjun and Cara Beltran. IGM runs a monthly two-day Bible School for island church leaders and works closely with Bohol Maranatha Christian Academy.

Email: iombohol@yahoo.com

Talibon, Bohol, Philippines

Bohol Maranatha Christian Academy

A thriving Christian school of ~160 students started by the McKays in 1990 at the request of the local church — providing quality Christian education and Australian sponsorship pathways.

Many poor farming and fishing families struggle to afford school fees. Through a child sponsorship scheme, GPC channels funds from Australian sponsors to help educate students and support teachers. Strong academic results now draw many non-believing families to enrol their children, creating wonderful evangelistic opportunities.

Nuba Mountains, South Sudan

Lael & Laura Luxford

Serving alongside a national team in the Nuba Mountains to shine for Christ (Phil 1:26; Matt 25:40) through disability care, a busy medical clinic, and local farming that feeds the hungry.

1. Caring for the disabled: Two all-day programs monthly serving 50 disabled people per day with transport, two meals, hygiene/health care, activities, the gospel, and take-home essentials.
2. Providing health care: Treating ~600 patients monthly from 40 villages plus antenatal care for ~125 women each month.
3. Feeding the hungry: Employing locals in need to cultivate sorghum, groundnuts, sesame seeds, and black-eyed beans for programs and hunger relief.

Kita Hiroshima, Hokkaido, Japan

Tony & Debbie Evans

Independent Baptist missionaries in Hokkaido since the early 1990s, where Suzuran Church now serves all ages alongside a small Bible institute training local believers and new missionaries.

Tony and Debbie have five adult children all living in different parts of Japan, two of whom assist with the ministry. Beginning through English classes, community involvement, and personal witnessing, Suzuran Church now gathers in its own premises with programs from newborn to elderly, while Tony and Debbie also mentor national pastors and incoming missionaries.

South Asia · Name withheld for security

M E (With Interserve)

“My passion is to live and work amongst the least resourced peoples in Islamic South Asia. As a biomedical engineer and educator, I am a witness of Jesus in fixing machines and training medical technicians.”

In my spare time, I like to hang out with friends at the gym, go hiking, and play guitar.”
